Order Lifecycle

  • NEW Stock reserved atomically on checkout
  • CONFIRMED Shop accepts within 30-min window
  • PACKING Delivery staff assigned (activeOrderId set)
  • OUT_FOR_DELIVERY Customer tracks live
  • DELIVERED Atomic claim → settlement fires

Cancellation Rules

  • Ghost cron NEW for >30 min → auto-cancel
  • Stock restore bulkWrite $inc: stock +qty
  • Refund Wallet refund txn if payment was PAID
  • Post-delivery Settlement reversal possible
  • Fake UTR Order cancelled + stock restored immediately

Settlement Details

  • Trigger DELIVERED state transition
  • Atomic findOneAndUpdate prevents race condition
  • Idempotent settle_order_{id} key in PlatformLedger
  • COD/Cash Settled on delivery without pre-payment

Shop Eligibility Gate

  • Subscription Active ShopSubscription required
  • KYC Verified — shop owner cannot self-approve
  • Status isOnline=true required to receive orders
  • Cron Expired subscription → forced offline in ≤10 min
